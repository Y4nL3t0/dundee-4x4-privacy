Account and data deletion

Dundee’s Games 4x4 — Dundee’s Games, developed by Yan Letourneau.
Last updated: October 29, 2026.

Request deletion of your game account

  1. Email yanletourneau+android@gmail.com with the subject Dundee’s Games 4x4 — deletion request.
  2. Say whether you want to delete your game account and associated data, or selected game data while keeping your account. For a partial request, specify the data concerned.
  3. Include your player nickname and, if available, the game account identifier supplied by the in-game request. You can submit a request here without reinstalling or signing in to the game.
  4. The developer will review the request, verify account ownership, explain any data that cannot be deleted, and confirm completion. An account identifier or nickname alone is not proof of ownership. If you cannot access the game, mention this so ownership verification can be discussed.

Email an account deletion request

If no email app opens, copy the address above into your email service. Opening this page or an email draft does not send a request or delete data. Never send passwords, verification codes, payment card numbers, purchase tokens or authentication tokens.

What is deleted

After ownership is verified, a full request covers the game’s identifiable Firebase Authentication account, its Firebase session records and identifiable multiplayer room/lobby/presence data held by the developer. Removing an affected multiplayer room can end that match. Data already received on other players’ devices cannot be remotely recalled.

For a partial request, the developer will confirm which requested records can be removed while keeping the game account. No additional active retention is planned for the Firebase records once the verified deletion has been completed. Processing is manual; this is not an automatic deletion within 90 days. Minimal request correspondence may be kept as necessary to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations; any applicable exception will be explained.

Local data and Google Play Games

Clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings removes local preferences, statistics and saved games on that device. Cloud data are separate and are not deleted merely by uninstalling the game.

Saved Games, achievements and leaderboards managed by Google Play Games also need to be addressed. Use Google Play Games settings to delete the data for Dundee’s Games 4x4, and include these data in your request so the developer can explain the applicable steps and limitations. See Google’s game-data deletion instructions. Requesting deletion does not delete your Google account. Signing in again can create a new game account and can restore independently retained Play Games data.

Purchases and third-party records

A deletion request does not refund or cancel your Google Play purchase. Google keeps purchase, security and other service records according to its own retention and legal requirements; see Google’s Privacy Policy. The developer does not operate a separate payment-card database. AdMob data and identifiers are managed under Google’s privacy controls and retention policies; a game-account deletion is not a promise to erase every record held independently by Google.

Contact: yanletourneau+android@gmail.com. See the Privacy Policy for all data categories and providers.
